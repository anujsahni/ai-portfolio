# Pricing policy 1 — Discount approval

*Altura Analytics pricing policy · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

Discounts are measured against list price for the first-year subscription.

| Discount | Who must approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account executive |
| More than 10%, up to 20% | Regional sales manager |
| More than 20%, up to 30% | VP of Sales |
| More than 30% | Chief Financial Officer |

**Rules**
- Get approval **before** the discount is quoted to the customer, in writing or verbally.
- A discount must be tied to something we get in return: a multi-year term, upfront payment, a case study, or signature by a stated date.
- Quarter-end pressure alone doesn't justify a higher tier. Approvers will ask what changed in the deal.
- Record the approved discount in the opportunity's Discount field and the approver in the description.

Discounts above 40% are not available.
