# Pricing policy 3 — Contract terms and payment

*Altura Analytics pricing policy · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

- **Standard term:** 12 months, billed annually upfront, net 30.
- **Multi-year:** 24 or 36 months may carry up to 5 extra percentage points of discount, within the approval tiers in policy 1.
- **Quarterly billing:** allowed for Enterprise customers with regional sales manager approval; no extra discount.
- **Price lock:** multi-year contracts lock the per-unit price; renewals of 12-month contracts may increase by up to 7%.
- **Non-standard terms** (termination for convenience, uncapped liability, custom data residency) need legal approval before they're offered.
