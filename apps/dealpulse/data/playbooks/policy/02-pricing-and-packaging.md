# Pricing policy 2 — Pricing and packaging

*Altura Analytics pricing policy · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

Altura sells annual subscriptions, priced by company size (segment) and product.

| Product | What it includes | Relative price |
|---|---|---|
| **Core** | Data platform, semantic layer, dashboards, alerting | 1.0× |
| **Governance add-on** | Lineage, access policies, audit trail | 0.55× Core |
| **AI add-on** | Natural-language questions, forecasting, anomaly detection | 0.75× Core |

- Add-ons require Core.
- Prices are list prices before any approved discount.
- Proof-of-value sandboxes are free for up to 30 days.
- Professional services (onboarding, data modelling) are quoted separately and aren't discounted beyond 10%.
