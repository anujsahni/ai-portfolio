# Pricing policy 4 — Forecast categories

*Altura Analytics sales operations · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

Every open opportunity has a forecast category, set by its owner and reviewed weekly with their manager.

| Category | Meaning | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | Real opportunity, outcome uncertain | Qualified pain and a next step |
| **Best Case** | Could close this quarter if things go well | Proposal sent; champion identified |
| **Commit** | Expected to close this quarter | Economic buyer engaged in the last 30 days; mutual action plan with dated steps; no open Sev-1/Sev-2 case on an expansion; discount (if any) approved |
| **Omitted** | Not counted this quarter | — |

A deal's stage sets its default probability in Salesforce (Prospecting 10% … Negotiation/Review 75%). Forecast categories are the owner's judgement and can differ from the stage. Managers should question Commit deals that lack the minimum evidence above.
