# Playbook 5 — Competitive deals

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

When another vendor is in the evaluation, win on the customer's decision criteria, not on price.

**First, find out:** which competitor, what they've shown, and which criteria the customer weighs most.

**Where we usually win:** governance and lineage, the semantic layer (one definition of each KPI), and time to value for finance teams.

**Where we usually lose:** when we never meet the economic buyer and the decision becomes a price comparison run by procurement.

**Tactics:**
- Offer a side-by-side session on the two criteria that matter most to them, using their data.
- Re-frame cost as three-year total cost of ownership, including the internal effort to maintain definitions and access rules.
- Bring a reference customer who switched from the same competitor.
- Don't lead with discounts. If price becomes the main issue, involve your manager before quoting; see the discount approval policy.

**Late entry:** a competitor appearing at Negotiation usually means procurement wants leverage. Confirm with the champion whether it's a real alternative.
