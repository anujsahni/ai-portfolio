# Playbook 3 — Finding and building a champion

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

A champion has influence, a personal stake in the outcome, and is willing to sell for us internally. A friendly contact who takes our calls is not automatically a champion.

**Test a champion with three asks:**
1. Ask them to share the internal decision process and who is involved.
2. Ask for a meeting with the economic buyer.
3. Ask them to present the business case internally, with our help.

If they say yes to all three, record them with the Champion contact role. If they decline, keep looking.

**When there is no champion:** the evaluation drifts, the team is "lukewarm", and nobody owns the decision. Widen the conversation: meet the leader of the team with the most pain, run a short workshop on their use case, and look for the person who asks the sharpest questions.

**Equip the champion:** a two-page business case with their metrics, a reference customer, a draft mutual action plan, and answers to the objections they'll hear from finance and IT.
