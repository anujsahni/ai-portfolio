# Playbook 2 — Executive alignment

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

Deals without an engaged executive sponsor close far less often, and they slip more. Our win/loss reviews show the economic buyer's absence as the most common reason a late-stage deal is lost or pushed.

**When to act:** no meeting with a C-level or VP on the customer side in the last 30 days, and the deal is at Proposal or later.

**How to get there:**
- Ask your champion directly: "Who signs off on this budget, and what do they need to see?" Offer to help write that material.
- Propose a 30-minute **executive alignment call**: their sponsor plus our regional sales manager or a VP. Agenda: business outcome, success metrics, timeline, risks.
- Bring one customer reference from the same industry and size.
- After the call, send a one-page summary of what was agreed and update the mutual action plan.

**Red flags:** "the budget will be decided later this quarter" with no date; the champion can't name the economic buyer; every meeting is with analysts or managers only.

**Forecast impact:** a deal with no executive engagement should be forecast as Best Case at most until the alignment call has happened.
