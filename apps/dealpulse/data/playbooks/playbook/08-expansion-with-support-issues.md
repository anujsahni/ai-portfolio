# Playbook 8 — Expansion deals with open support issues

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

Existing customers buy again when the current product works for them. An open Sev-1 or Sev-2 support case usually freezes an expansion until it is resolved.

**Before every expansion call,** check the account's open cases. Never be surprised by a customer's open issue.

**If a severe case is open:**
- Acknowledge it first in the conversation. Don't pitch past it.
- Agree a resolution plan with the support lead, with a date, and share it with the customer.
- Ask the customer success manager to join the next call.
- Expect the close date to depend on the fix; set it accordingly rather than letting it slip repeatedly.

**After resolution,** ask the customer to confirm the fix, then resume the expansion with a short recap of value delivered so far.

**Forecast impact:** don't forecast an expansion as Commit while a Sev-1 or Sev-2 case is open on the account.
