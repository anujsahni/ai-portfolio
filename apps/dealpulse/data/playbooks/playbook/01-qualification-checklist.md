# Playbook 1 — Qualification checklist

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

A deal is qualified when we can answer each question below with evidence from a conversation, not a guess. Review the checklist before moving a deal past Needs Analysis and again before forecasting it as Commit.

1. **Pain.** What business problem costs them money or time today, in their words? Example: "month-end close takes nine days."
2. **Metrics.** How will they measure success? Agree two or three measurable outcomes before the proof of value.
3. **Economic buyer.** Who can approve the budget, and have we met them? A director who "will take it upstairs" is not the economic buyer.
4. **Decision process.** What steps happen between "yes, we like it" and a signature: security, legal, procurement, board?
5. **Decision criteria.** What will they compare vendors on? If a competitor is involved, which criteria favour us?
6. **Champion.** Who inside the customer wants us to win and is actively selling for us when we're not in the room?
7. **Competition.** Who else is in the evaluation, including "do nothing" and an internal build?
8. **Timeline.** What event forces a decision this quarter? Without one, expect the close date to slip.

**Forecasting rule:** don't forecast a deal as Commit unless items 3, 4 and 6 are confirmed and dated in the opportunity's activity history.
