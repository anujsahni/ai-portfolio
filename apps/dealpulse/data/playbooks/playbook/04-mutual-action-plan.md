# Playbook 4 — Mutual action plan

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

A mutual action plan (MAP) is a shared, dated list of the steps both sides must complete to go live by the customer's target date. It turns "we'll get back to you" into commitments.

**Build it at Proposal stage, with the champion.** Work backwards from their go-live date: signature, procurement, legal, security review, stakeholder sign-off, proof-of-value results.

**Each step needs** an owner on their side, an owner on ours and a date.

**Use it to protect the close date.** When a step slips, ask why the same day and agree a recovery date. A close date that moves without a matching change in the MAP is a warning sign.

**Common slip points:** legal redlines (allow two rounds), security questionnaires (send our SOC 2 Type II report early), and procurement onboarding (vendor forms, insurance certificate, W-9).

**Repeated slips:** if a deal's close date has moved twice or more, review it with your manager before forecasting it again. Re-confirm the compelling event and the economic buyer's commitment; often one of them has gone.
