# Playbook 6 — Stalled deals and customers going dark

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

A deal is stalling when activity falls off: fewer meetings, slower replies, unanswered emails. Two weeks with no reply at Proposal stage or later needs action, not another "just checking in" email.

**Diagnose first.** Common causes: a priority changed, the budget was frozen, the champion left or lost influence, or a competitor got ahead.

**Re-engage with value, not pressure:**
- Send something useful: a short analysis of their sample data, a relevant customer story, or the answer to an open question.
- Go wider: contact another stakeholder you've met, or ask your manager to reach their executive sponsor.
- Offer a clear choice: "Should we pause until next quarter, or is there a way to keep this moving?" A clear "not now" is better for the forecast than silence.

**Forecast impact:** move a dark deal out of Commit until two-way contact is restored and the next step is dated.
