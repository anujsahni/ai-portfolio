# Playbook 7 — Security and legal reviews

*Altura Analytics sales playbook · fictional, for the DealPulse demo*

Security and legal reviews are the most common cause of late-quarter slips. Start them early, not after verbal agreement.

**Security review**
- Send our SOC 2 Type II report, penetration test summary and data processing addendum as soon as the customer starts the technical evaluation.
- Answer questionnaires within two business days; involve the sales engineer for architecture questions (SSO, encryption keys, data residency).

**Legal review**
- Send our standard MSA at Proposal stage. Typical redlines: limitation of liability, data residency, renewal notice.
- Plan for two rounds of redlines. Changes outside our standard fallback positions need legal approval and can take a week.

**Watch for:** "legal review slipped" more than once, or security asking new questions late in the quarter. Both suggest the deal isn't a priority yet; re-confirm the timeline with the executive sponsor.
