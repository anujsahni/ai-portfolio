# Downtown Santa Cruz: art and coffee

This sample guide suggests downtown Santa Cruz for a café stop along Pacific Avenue and a visit to the Museum of Art & History (MAH). It is a good indoor-friendly block on a foggy or cool day.

Museum admission, exhibits, and café hours are not verified. Use the guide as a preference cue and confirm details before travel.
