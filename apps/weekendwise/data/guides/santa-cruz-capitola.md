# Capitola Village

This sample guide suggests Capitola, a small beach village just east of Santa Cruz, for a slow stroll among colorful cottages, small shops, and a short pier. It works as a half-day add-on with a coffee or ice-cream stop.

Parking is limited on busy weekends and a car or bus ride is needed from Santa Cruz. Businesses and hours are not verified here.
