# Lodging estimates for a weekend sample

The sample budget treats lodging as a single estimated line rather than a specific hotel. For demo purposes, a modest room for two nights can be shown as about $180–$260 in total for the party, and a budget-conscious stay as about $120–$180.

These figures are fabricated planning fixtures. The app does not search, hold, or book rooms, and real rates vary by date, location, and availability.
