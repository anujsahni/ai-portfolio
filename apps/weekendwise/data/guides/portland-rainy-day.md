# Rainy-day planning in Portland

Portland often sees drizzle from fall through spring. When the simulated conditions are rainy, keep the traveler's interests and swap outdoor garden time for indoor options such as the art museum, Powell's, or a café, then move outdoor blocks to a drier day.

Mark weather results as simulated when they come from the demo tool. Do not claim live forecasts or venue availability.
