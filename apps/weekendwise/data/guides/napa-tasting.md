# Planning a wine-tasting day

This sample note suggests limiting a tasting day to two or three wineries with a long lunch in between. Many wineries require advance tasting reservations, so the plan should say to book directly rather than claim a slot is available.

Every tasting plan must include a designated driver, tour, or rideshare and must not encourage drinking and driving. This demo makes no reservations; tasting fees are fabricated fixtures, shown as roughly $40–$75 per person.
