# An evening of live music

When a traveler lists live music as an interest, a planner can reserve an open evening block and suggest checking current venue listings. The draft should not name a specific show, set time, or ticket price as if it were confirmed.

This demo corpus has no event calendar and cannot buy or hold tickets. Present live music as an optional idea and point the traveler to official venue listings.
