# Food-cart pods for a casual meal

For a traveler who lists great food, this sample guide suggests a lunch or dinner at one of Portland's food-cart pods, where a group can each pick something different. It is a flexible, budget-friendly option.

For demo budgeting only, a cart meal can be shown as roughly $12–$20 per person. Specific carts, locations, and hours change often and are not included in this fixture.
