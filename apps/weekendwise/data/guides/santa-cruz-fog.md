# Fog and coastal weather in Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz mornings are often foggy, especially in summer, and usually clear by midday. When the simulated conditions show fog, schedule indoor or town activities in the morning and move coastal walks and beach time to the afternoon.

Ocean conditions and cliff paths can be hazardous. Mark weather as simulated, and tell travelers to follow posted beach and surf guidance.
