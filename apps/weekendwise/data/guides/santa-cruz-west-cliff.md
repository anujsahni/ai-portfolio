# West Cliff Drive to Natural Bridges

This sample outdoor guide suggests an easy oceanfront walk or bike ride along West Cliff Drive, ending at Natural Bridges State Beach. It fits a relaxed morning once any fog lifts.

Cliff paths can be windy and beach access can change. Conditions are simulated in this demo; confirm current park and safety guidance before travel.
