# Getting around Portland without a car

This sample transit note suggests MAX light rail from the airport to downtown, and the streetcar or buses between central neighborhoods. Group stops on the same side of the Willamette River to cut transfer time.

No schedules, fares, or service alerts are verified in this fixture. Use official TriMet information before travel.
