# Portland budget and lodging estimates

For demo purposes, a modest room for two nights can be shown as about $170–$250 total for the party, and local transit as about $10–$15 per person per day. Oregon has no general sales tax, but lodging taxes still apply.

All amounts are fabricated fixtures, not current prices. Show them as labeled estimates, itemize categories, and flag any plan that exceeds the traveler's budget.
