# Beach Boardwalk and Wharf

This sample note frames the Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk, a classic seaside amusement park, and the nearby wharf as a fun half-day. A planner can suggest a stroll and a few rides rather than a full day of attractions.

Ride prices, seasonal hours, and parking are not verified. Summer weekends are busy; suggest arriving early and checking official information.
