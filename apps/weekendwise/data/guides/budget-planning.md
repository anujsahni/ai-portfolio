# Budget-aware itinerary planning

Treat the user’s budget as a hard planning constraint for the sample. Show a clearly labeled estimate, itemize the categories used, and leave an uncertainty buffer. If the estimated total is over budget, say so and suggest lower-cost substitutions rather than labeling the plan “on track.”

All demo amounts are fabricated fixtures. They are not current admission, dining, lodging, or transportation prices.
