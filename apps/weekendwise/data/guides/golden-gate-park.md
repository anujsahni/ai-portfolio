# Golden Gate Park: an easy green break

This sample guide treats Golden Gate Park as a flexible outdoor block that pairs well with a nearby museum visit. A planner can suggest a short, unhurried stroll before or after an indoor activity rather than a long hike.

Paths, gardens, facilities, and events are not verified in this fixture. In poor simulated conditions, shorten or drop the outdoor block and keep the indoor option.
