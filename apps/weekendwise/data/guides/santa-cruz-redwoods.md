# Redwoods at Henry Cowell

This sample guide suggests a short, easy redwood loop at Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park, a short drive inland from town. It pairs well with a slow morning and a picnic.

A car is usually needed. Trail status, parking fees, and hours are not verified in this fixture; check official state park information.
