# Art in Napa Valley

This sample note suggests pairing wine country with art: several wineries display sculpture or painting collections, the di Rosa Center for Contemporary Art sits in the Carneros area, and downtown Napa has small galleries and public art along the river.

Gallery hours, admission, and winery art access are not verified and some need reservations. Check official information before making plans.
