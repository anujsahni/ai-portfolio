# Accessibility and trip constraints in Napa Valley

If a traveler shares an accessibility or mobility need, keep it throughout planning and ask which accommodations matter when unclear. In Napa Valley, details such as winery grounds, tasting rooms, and transport between towns vary by place and should be confirmed in advance.

This demo corpus does not verify accessible entrances, elevators, path grades, or services. Say so, and direct the traveler to confirm details with official providers.
