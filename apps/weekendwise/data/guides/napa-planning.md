# Napa Valley budget and getting around

For demo purposes, a modest room for two nights can be shown as about $350–$550 total for the party, often higher during harvest (roughly August to October). Wineries are spread out, so most visitors drive, book a tour or driver, or use rideshare; VINE Transit buses connect the towns.

All amounts are fabricated fixtures, not current prices. Napa trips are usually more expensive than city weekends, so flag over-budget plans and suggest lower-cost swaps such as a picnic or a single tasting.
