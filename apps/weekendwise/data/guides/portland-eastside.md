# Eastside neighborhoods: Hawthorne, Division, and Mississippi

This sample guide suggests an eastside afternoon for local finds: vintage and record shops on Hawthorne, cafés and bakeries along Division, or small shops on Mississippi Avenue. Pick one street and wander rather than trying to cover all three.

Shops, hours, and prices are not verified in this fixture. Buses connect these streets to downtown; check current TriMet information.
