# Accessibility and trip constraints in Santa Cruz

If a traveler shares an accessibility or mobility need, keep it throughout planning and ask which accommodations matter when unclear. In Santa Cruz, details such as beach access and cliff paths vary by place and should be confirmed in advance.

This demo corpus does not verify accessible entrances, elevators, path grades, or services. Say so, and direct the traveler to confirm details with official providers.
