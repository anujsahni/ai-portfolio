# Santa Cruz budget and getting around

For demo purposes, a modest room for two nights can be shown as about $220–$320 total for the party, with higher rates on summer weekends. Downtown, the wharf, and the boardwalk are walkable; a car helps for redwoods and outer beaches, and Santa Cruz METRO buses cover town.

All amounts are fabricated fixtures, not current prices. Show them as labeled estimates and flag any plan that exceeds the traveler's budget.
