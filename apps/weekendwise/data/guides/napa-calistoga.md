# Calistoga: hot springs and a spa break

This sample guide suggests Calistoga, at the north end of the valley, for a spa or mud-bath break and a short main-street wander. It is a good indoor-friendly option on a very hot afternoon.

Spa services usually need reservations; this demo does not book them. Prices and hours are not verified.
