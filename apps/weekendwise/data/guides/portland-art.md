# Art in Portland: museum and Alberta Arts

This sample note uses the Portland Art Museum downtown as an indoor activity for an art-focused or rainy day, and the Alberta Arts District as an optional gallery-and-mural stroll when conditions are dry.

Admission, exhibits, and gallery hours are intentionally not treated as live facts. Check official museum information before making plans.
