# Washington Park gardens

This sample outdoor guide suggests a relaxed garden block in Washington Park, home to the Portland Japanese Garden and the International Rose Test Garden. Plan it for a dry part of the day and keep the walk short.

Portland often has drizzle from fall through spring. If simulated conditions are rainy, move this block and use an indoor option. Garden admission, hours, and paths are not verified here.
