# Casual food in Santa Cruz

This sample food guide suggests casual seafood on or near the wharf, a taqueria lunch, or a downtown dinner along Pacific Avenue. Santa Cruz meals tend to be relaxed, so a planner can leave the choice open.

For demo budgeting only, a casual meal can be shown as roughly $15–$35 per person. Specific restaurants, hours, and prices are not verified in this fixture.
