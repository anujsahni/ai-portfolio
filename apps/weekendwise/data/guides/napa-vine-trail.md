# Napa Valley Vine Trail

This sample outdoor guide suggests a short walk or bike ride on a section of the Napa Valley Vine Trail, a paved path along the valley. Plan it for the cooler morning in summer.

Summer afternoons can be hot. Trail sections, bike rentals, and conditions are not verified; check official trail information.
